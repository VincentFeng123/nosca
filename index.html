05 — Questions

A few things you might be wondering.

Does ReelAI make new videos?

No. It finds useful moments in real videos and opens them at the right timestamp, so you keep the original explanation and speaker.

What can I start with?

A topic, a question, a YouTube video, playlist, or channel, or your own PDF, DOCX, and TXT study material.

Why not just ask for a summary?

A summary can flatten the example or turn of phrase that made an idea click. ReelAI takes you back to the original words and the moment around them.

What if the video has no captions?

ReelAI can create timestamped transcript cues first, then use those cues to find and play the relevant moment.
